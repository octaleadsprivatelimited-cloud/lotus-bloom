# Lotus Bloom

Lovable Prompt — Lotus Home Inspection Website

Copy everything below into Lovable as your project prompt.

Build a modern, professional marketing website for Lotus Home Inspection, a home inspection company. Use a clean, trustworthy visual style: primarily white/light backgrounds, a deep corporate blue as the primary brand color, a secondary accent (e.g. teal or soft gold) for CTAs and highlights, generous whitespace, rounded cards, subtle shadows, and clear sans-serif typography (e.g. Inter or similar). The site should feel credible, calm, and reassuring — like a company homebuyers can trust with a major financial decision. Fully responsive (mobile-first), fast-loading, with smooth scroll and subtle fade/slide-in animations on scroll.

Lotus Home Inspection provides inspections statewide. Do not lock content to a single city — use language like "serving homeowners across the state" / "wherever you are in [State]" and include a "Service Areas" section listing several major cities/regions (use placeholder city names I can edit later).

Site Structure & Pages

1. Header / Navigation

Logo left ("Lotus Home Inspection" wordmark + simple lotus flower icon mark)

Nav links: Home, About Us, Services, Service Areas, Resources/Blog, Contact

Prominent phone number and a "Book Inspection" button (accent color) on the right

Sticky on scroll, transparent-to-solid transition

2. Hero Section

Full-width hero with a background image/video of a home exterior or an inspector at work

Large headline: something like "Statewide Home Inspection Services You Can Trust"

Subheadline: one to two sentences on thorough, certified, unbiased inspections before you buy, sell, or build

Two CTAs: primary "Book an Inspection" button, secondary "Call Now" with phone icon

Small trust row beneath hero: certifications/associations logos placeholder, years in business, homes inspected count (use editable placeholder stats, e.g. "15+ Years Experience", "5,000+ Inspections Completed", "100% Client Satisfaction")

3. About / Intro Section

Two-column layout: image of inspector on left, text on right

Heading: "Home Inspection Services You Can Rely On, Statewide"

Short paragraph explaining who Lotus Home Inspection is, what makes them different (certified engineers/inspectors, detailed photo reports, honest recommendations)

3 short checklist bullets (e.g. "Certified & Licensed Inspectors", "Detailed Reports With Photos", "Same-Day or Next-Day Reports")

CTA button: "More About Us"

4. "How We Help" / Process Strip

4-column icon row (Inspect → Detect → Protect → Report), each with an icon, short title, and 1-line description — mirrors a simple 4-step trust-building flow

Light background band to visually separate from surrounding sections

5. Services Section (core focus)

Feature these services as individual cards (icon, title, 2–3 line description, "Learn More" link) in a responsive grid:

General Home Inspection — full top-to-bottom inspection covering roof, foundation, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structure

New Construction Inspection — phase inspections during construction (pre-pour, pre-drywall, final) to catch issues before they're sealed behind walls

Pre-Listing Inspection — inspection for sellers before listing, to identify and address issues proactively and avoid surprises during buyer negotiations

Re-Inspection — follow-up inspection to confirm repairs were completed correctly

Radon / Mold Testing (optional add-on card, marked "Available Add-On")

Termite / Pest Inspection (optional add-on card, marked "Available Add-On")

Section heading: "Comprehensive Inspection Services, Wherever You're Buying or Building" Include a closing CTA banner beneath the grid: "Not sure which inspection you need? Talk to our team" with a Contact/Book button.

6. Why Choose Us

Alternating icon + text rows or a 3-column layout covering:

Statewide coverage & flexible scheduling

Certified, experienced inspectors

Clear, easy-to-read digital reports with photos, delivered fast

Honest, unbiased assessments — no conflicts of interest

Include a small stats bar again (years of experience, inspections completed, satisfaction rate) styled as large bold numbers with labels

7. What a Home Inspection Covers

Visual grid/checklist section (6 items with icons) covering: Foundation & Structure, Roof, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Moisture/Dampness — matching how thorough the inspection is

Short intro paragraph above the grid

8. Service Areas

Heading: "Proudly Serving Homeowners Across [State]"

Grid or pill/tag list of multiple placeholder city names (editable), implying statewide reach

Optional simple map graphic or state outline illustration (placeholder)

9. Testimonials

Carousel or 3-card grid of client testimonials (name, role e.g. "Home Buyer", "Realtor", short quote, star rating)

Use 3–4 realistic placeholder testimonials

10. Blog / Resources Preview

Section heading: "Home Inspection Tips & Resources"

3-card preview grid (image, category tag, title, date, "Read More") linking to a future blog page

"View All Articles" button

11. FAQ Section

Accordion-style FAQ with 5–6 questions such as: What is a home inspection and why do I need one? Who conducts the inspections? When should I schedule one? How long does an inspection take? Do you inspect new construction? What's included in the report?

12. Final CTA / Book Inspection Banner

Full-width colored band (primary blue) with white text

Heading: "Ready to Schedule Your Inspection?"

Short line + phone number + "Book Now" button (contrasting/accent color)

13. Footer

4-column layout: Logo + short tagline + social icons | Quick Links (Home, About, Services, Contact) | Services list (links to each service) | Contact info (phone, email, "Statewide Service" note) + a simple inline contact/newsletter form

Bottom bar: copyright, privacy policy / terms links

Additional Pages (basic versions, linked from nav)

About Us page — team story, mission, certifications

Services page — expanded detail on each service listed above, each as its own section or sub-page

Contact Us page — contact form (name, email, phone, property address, preferred date, message), phone/email, and a "statewide service" note instead of a single office map

Book Inspection page — simple booking form (name, contact info, property address, inspection type dropdown, preferred date/time, notes) with a confirmation message on submit

Technical Notes

Use a component-based structure (Hero, Navbar, ServiceCard, TestimonialCard, FAQItem, Footer, etc.) so sections are easy to edit later

Use placeholder stock-style imagery (home exteriors, inspectors working, families) — I will replace with real photos later

Make all stats, testimonials, and city names easy to find/edit as they're placeholder content

Ensure strong color contrast and accessible font sizes throughout

Include a favicon and page title "Lotus Home Inspection | Statewide Home Inspection Services"
